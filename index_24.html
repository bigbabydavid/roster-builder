Team Autosaves in this browser Syncing…

The roster pool syncs with MLB's live 40-man roster automatically on load and every hour after — new call-ups or trades show up in the pool, and injury status stays current. It only ever adds players or refreshes their name/number/injury flag; it never touches where you've placed someone or any Lock/Bubble you've set. Drag a player tile between the roster columns and the pool below to change where they sit — a thin orange line shows exactly where they'll land as you drag, so you can drop them at any precise spot rather than just the end. Each tile has two small buttons on its right edge: the top dot cycles blank → green (Lock) → yellow (Bubble) → blank again with each click; the + below it is a separate on/off toggle for currently injured. Neither moves the player anywhere.
